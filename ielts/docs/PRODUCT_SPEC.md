# Product Specification: AI-Powered IELTS Preparation Platform

## 1. Product Overview
An intelligent, production-ready SaaS platform built for IELTS candidates (targeting Uzbek & global learners) to practice Reading, Listening, Writing, and Speaking with real-time AI feedback, diagnostic testing, mock exams, and local subscription integrations.

## 2. Target Audience & Roles
- **Visitor:** Landing page, sample diagnostics, pricing tiers.
- **Learner:** Diagnostic tests, full mock exams, personalized study roadmap, AI Writing/Speaking evaluations, mistake notebook, spaced repetition vocabulary.
- **Admin:** Question bank management, audio/passage content CMS, payment logs, user analytics.

## 3. Core Modules
- **Diagnostic Engine:** Multi-skill baseline test estimating starting bands.
- **Reading Engine:** 13+ standard IELTS question types, passage highlighting, objective auto-grading.
- **Listening Engine:** Audio player with timing constraints, audio transcripts, auto-scoring.
- **Writing Assessment:** Tasks 1 & 2 editor, autosave, AI grading according to 4 official criteria (TR/TA, CC, LR, GRA) with actionable rewrite suggestions.
- **Speaking Engine:** Real-time AI Examiner (WebRTC / Voice AI) for Parts 1, 2, and 3 with audio analysis for pronunciation and fluency.
- **Monetization (Uzbekistan):** Free, Premium, and Pro tiers integrating Payme, Click, and Uzum via webhook-driven billing.
